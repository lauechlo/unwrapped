## Pattern Label Guidelines

**CRITICAL: Labels must be screenshot-worthy**

Pattern labels should:
- **Include artist/track names** - "The Sabrina Carpenter Chokehold" not "The Loyalist"
- **Use Gen Z slang** - "era", "chokehold", "stranglehold", "hyperfixation"
- **Reference numbers** - "6-Artist Monopoly", "17-Track Vault", "4/4 Sunday Ritual"
- **Be culturally specific** - "(Taylor's Version)", "Wicked Era"
- **Feel personal** - make user go "oh shit that's me"

**Gen Z Vocabulary:**
- "chokehold", "stranglehold", "vice grip"
- "era", "hyperfixation", "fixation era"
- "main character energy"
- "emotional support [artist]"
- "parasocial moment"
- "witness protection" (for vaulted songs)
